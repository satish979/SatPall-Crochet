Terms & Conditions

Please review our terms of service regarding handcrafted items, custom orders, and payments.

1. Nature of Handmade Creations

Every item at Loomelle Crochet is made individually by hand. Slight variations in stitch tension, dimensions (±1-2 cm), or color shades due to different yarn dye lots are natural hallmarks of handmade art and make each piece uniquely yours.

2. Order Acceptance & Customization

Custom color requests are subject to yarn availability. Once custom production has begun, changes or cancellations may not be possible. We will always communicate with you regarding processing times.

3. Pricing & Payments

All prices are listed in Indian Rupees (₹). Payments are securely processed through encrypted payment gateways (Razorpay) or verified Cash on Delivery (COD).

4. Shipping & Damaged Deliveries

We inspect and gift-pack every order thoroughly. If an item arrives damaged due to postal transit, please notify us within 48 hours of delivery with photo proof for immediate resolution.
