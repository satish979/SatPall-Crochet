Privacy Policy

How Loomelle Crochet respects, protects, and handles your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect essential details required to process, fulfill, and deliver your orders — such as your name, delivery address, email, and phone number.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to send order status updates, OTP login codes, shipping notifications, and custom inquiry replies. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketers.

3. Payment Security

We do not store your credit card, debit card, or net banking passwords. All online payments are securely processed through industry-standard encrypted channels (Razorpay).

4. Contacting Us

If you have questions regarding your stored profile data or wish to delete your account, please reach out to us at loomellecrochet.support@gmail.com.
